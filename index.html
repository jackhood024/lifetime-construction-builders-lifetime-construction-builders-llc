Lifetime Construction Builders LLC: A Plain Glossary for Bryant Homeowners

Roofing has its own vocabulary. This glossary defines the terms a Bryant homeowner meets on a roof job, and how each shows up when Lifetime Construction Builders handles the work.

Free roof inspection

Someone walks the roof, checks the shingles, flashing and vents, and reports its condition. Lifetime Construction offers it before any money changes hands, and photos of the damage are taken for the adjuster.

Written estimate

A document that names materials by product line and lists tear-off, underlayment, flashing, vents and cleanup. At Lifetime Construction the written price does not change afterward.

Tear-off

Removing the old roofing down to the deck before anything new goes on.

Underlayment

The layer between the deck and the shingles that sheds water getting past them.

Flashing

The metal pieces that seal the roof at chimneys, walls and valleys.

Decking

The boards under the roofing. A good estimate says what happens if rotted decking turns up at tear-off.

Ridge cap

The pieces that cover the peak of the roof.

Emergency tarp

A temporary cover that keeps water out until a repair can be made. After hail or high wind, a crew comes out the same day, within a few hours, to tarp when water is getting in.

Adjuster

The insurance company's representative who inspects storm damage and sets the claim. Lifetime Construction helps homeowners through the claim when a storm did the damage.

Insurance claim help

The paperwork and inspection support that comes with a storm claim. At Lifetime Construction it is part of the service, not a separate charge.

Atlas Preferred Contractor

A certification tier from the shingle maker Atlas. Lifetime Construction holds it.

Atlas Pinnacle Pristine Authorized Installer

Authorization from Atlas to install its Pinnacle Pristine line. A homeowner can confirm it on Atlas's own site, not on a truck door.

Class 3 and Class 4 impact resistance

Ratings for how well a shingle takes a hit from hail. Atlas Pinnacle Pristine carries Class 3, and Pinnacle Impact carries Class 4.

Stone coated steel

A metal roofing product with a stone granule finish, made by DECRA, TILCOR and Westlake Royal. Lifetime Construction installs it.

Standing seam

Metal roofing with raised interlocking seams and hidden fasteners, one of the metal options offered.

R-panel

A ribbed metal panel with exposed fasteners. Lifetime Construction installs and repairs it.

Transferable warranty

A guarantee that passes to the next owner of the house. Lifetime Construction gives a lifetime transferable warranty on every installation.

General liability coverage

Insurance that pays if the crew damages property. A homeowner can ask for the certificate to be sent straight from the insurance agent.

Workers' compensation

Insurance that covers a worker hurt on the roof, which otherwise can become the homeowner's problem.

Magnet sweep

Passing a magnet over the lawn and drive to pick up nails. Lifetime Construction does this before the crew leaves, which is why Google reviews keep mentioning the clean yard.
